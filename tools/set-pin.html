Why a passphrase and not four digits?
The line above is a one-way fingerprint of what you typed, so a student reading your page source sees only the fingerprint. But that fingerprint is public too, and a determined person could sit and test guesses against it. Four digits would take seconds. Three or four unrelated words would take longer than anyone's patience. Avoid your name, the school's name, and anything a class could guess in ten tries.
Write it down somewhere safe. There is no way to recover a passphrase from the fingerprint — that is the entire point of it. If you forget it, you will need to come back here and generate a new line.
