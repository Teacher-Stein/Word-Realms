How To Play

  • Choose a hero at the start of each run - each begins with a different advantage.
  • The game picks one student per turn. Use Reroll if they're absent.
  • Fight rooms take 2 correct answers, Elite rooms take 5. Elites always drop a relic.
  • Watch the monster's intent underneath it - it tells you what is coming and when. Its clock counts down one step per answer from anyone in the class, not only when you answer wrongly.
  • Last Stand. The first time the party runs out of hearts, one student gets a single sudden-death question. Answer it and the party gets back up with one heart. Miss it and the run is over. Once per run only.
  • Grammar questions pay more shards but cost 2 hearts if you get them wrong. Vocabulary costs 1.
  • Brace to defend: you still answer a question, but a correct answer blocks the incoming attack instead of damaging the monster, and clears any debuff.
  • Stakes. Before the options appear, decide how much you are putting on the answer. SAFE plays normally. RISKY pays double shards and makes a mistake cost double. Backing yourself when you know it is the whole skill.
  • Focus — once per fight, the whole class answers together. Get it right and 2 answers are struck off the monster's clock. It buys time, never damage, so the fight runs longer.
  • At a Campfire there is time for one thing only: Mend hearts, Repair shields, or Sharpen for a permanent extra heart.
  • Shields (blue) soak damage before your hearts do and do NOT come back on their own. Only a Campfire Repair, a Patch Kit or better armour restores them.
  • On a question whose clue alone tells you what to say, RISKY goes further: the options vanish and you say the answer out loud for triple shards. It is never offered where the options ARE the question. Nothing in this game ever makes a fight shorter — the questions are the point.
  • Elite rooms ask harder questions — they test whether you can use the language, not just recognise it. Wrong costs 2 hearts; right pays the most shards in the game. The Boss asks them too.
  • You can open the pack during a fight. The student still answers their question, but the party doesn't attack that turn.
  • Watch the countdown on the monster's intent. It counts in answers from anybody, not just your own turns, and turns red when the next answer sets it off.
  • Team Up brings in a partner, but the monster recovers HP while you confer.
  • Three correct answers in a row blocks the next attack; five gives shards and a potion.
  • Shops appear deeper in the map, once you have shards to spend.
  • Between runs, spend banked Ember in the Ember Forge on permanent upgrades for your class. A lost run still buys something.
  • The Boss tests everything the class hasn't faced yet.
  • Lose all hearts a second time and the run ends - but the party banks Ember or keeps a relic for next time.
