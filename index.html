How To Play

  • The game picks one student per turn. That student makes ONE choice: which room to enter, or which answer to give. Use Reroll if they're absent.
  • Fight rooms take 2 correct answers to clear. Elite rooms take 5 — big rewards, big commitment.
  • A wrong answer lets the monster attack: the party loses a heart.
  • Team Up lets a student bring in a partner, but the monster gains 1 HP while you're talking. Once per monster.
  • Rest heals. Treasure gives shards or a relic. Event is a story choice. Shop sells relics and potions for Knowledge Shards.
  • Safe Path has no danger and no reward - but it's the one place the party can stop and drink a potion.
  • Open Inventory any time from the map to see every relic and potion the party is carrying, and what each one does.
  • The Boss tests every word and grammar point the class hasn't faced yet, so nothing gets skipped for good.
  • Lose all hearts and the run resets with a brand-new map — but the party keeps a relic or banks Ember for next time.
